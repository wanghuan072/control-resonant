# CONTROL Resonant Guide

[CONTROL Resonant Guide](https://controlresonant.org/) is an independent, English-language companion for players following Remedy Entertainment's CONTROL Resonant. The game follows Dylan Faden into a Manhattan altered by forces that do not obey ordinary rules. Combat centers on the transforming Aberrant weapon, supernatural abilities and fast movement between encounters.

The site is organized around the questions players actually ask: when and where can I play, how does a system work, who is this character, and where should I look next? Before release, information about exact routes, rewards and performance is necessarily limited. We do not present guesses as completed-game walkthroughs.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to play CONTROL first?

It can help you understand Jesse, Dylan and the Federal Bureau of Control, but our [getting-started guide](https://controlresonant.org/guides/getting-started) explains the background most relevant to Resonant.

### Is this an official CONTROL website?

No. This is an independent fan site, not an official Remedy Entertainment publication. Game names, marks and imagery belong to their respective owners.

### Does the Wiki include every mission, boss and item?

Not yet. The [Wiki](https://controlresonant.org/wiki) covers named and documented information. Full routes, exact rewards and complete lists require verification against the released game.
